Slice Business Credit Card preview

Slice is preparing a business credit card for sole proprietors. The pre-launch material promises no joining or annual fee, up to 2% cashback, zero forex markup and up to 45 days of interest-free credit. The Slice team has confirmed a December 2026 launch; the full terms aren’t out yet.

Worth watching, not yet judging

A lifetime-free business card with up to 2% cashback and zero forex markup sounds promising for sole proprietors. Its real value depends on the cashback exclusions, which Slice hasn’t published.

  • Join the waitlist if you run a sole proprietorship with regular business spends, especially in foreign currency. With no fee, there’s little to lose.

  • Don’t count on 2% for taxes, GST, utilities or advertising yet. If those are excluded, the headline rate is worth much less than it looks.

  • Before you apply, read which categories earn 2%, the monthly cashback cap and whether a Slice current account is required.

Cashback: up to 2% on business spends

The headline is up to 2% cashback. At that rate, ₹1 lakh of eligible spend would earn up to ₹2,000.

Slice hasn’t said:

  • Whether 2% applies to every eligible purchase or only to some categories
  • The monthly or statement-cycle cashback limit
  • Whether GST, income-tax, utility and government payments count
  • Whether advertising, software subscriptions and other business costs earn cashback
  • Any minimum transaction amount or spend requirement

Read 2% as a ceiling

Until the terms are out, treat 2% as the most the card could pay, not a rate you’ll get on every transaction.

Zero forex markup

Slice advertises a 0% forex markup. That could make the card useful for international business costs, such as software subscriptions, online services and overseas suppliers.

The final terms should also say whether international spends earn cashback and whether any network or currency-conversion charges apply. The images show both Visa and RuPay cards, but Slice hasn’t said how each one will be offered.

Up to 45 days of interest-free credit

Purchases could get up to 45 days without interest, which helps when your customers pay you after your own expenses are due.

The real interest-free period depends on when in the billing cycle you spend and on the payment due date. Interest rates and late-payment charges for this card haven’t been announced.

Who can apply?

The first waitlist is for sole proprietorships. Slice already offers business current accounts on its Slice Business banking platform, and this card extends that.

Still unclear: income requirements, the business documents needed, the credit score needed, and whether you must hold a Slice current account.

Questions

When does the Slice Business Credit Card launch?

In December 2026, as confirmed by the Slice team. The exact date hasn’t been announced.

Can I apply for the Slice Business Credit Card now?

Not yet. The card launches in December 2026, as confirmed by the Slice team. Until then only the waitlist is open, on Slice’s business banking website. Avoid unofficial links that claim to take applications.

Is the Slice Business Credit Card really lifetime free?

That’s what the pre-launch material says: no joining fee and no annual fee. Check the final terms for other charges, such as interest and late fees.

Will every spend earn 2% cashback?

Not known. Slice advertises “up to 2%” and hasn’t published which spends qualify, so some may earn less or nothing.

Can private limited companies apply?

The first waitlist is for sole proprietorships. Slice hasn’t said when other business types can apply.
