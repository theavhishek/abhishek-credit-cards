Kredit.Pe YES BANK Max Credit Card: the full review

A lifetime-free RuPay card advertising 5% cashback, 3% to 5% on UPI and up to ₹1,000 to start. The catch: the cashback stays inside Kredit.Pe, and voucher fees and redemption charges eat into it.

The verdict: attractive numbers, weak redemption

5% on a lifetime-free card sounds great, but it’s Kredit.Pe cashback, not statement credit. You spend it on vouchers, gold or travel inside the app, where premium voucher fees and redemption charges cut its value. Not recommended as a primary cashback card.

  • Fits if: you want a free RuPay card for UPI and already buy the vouchers, gold or flights Kredit.Pe sells.

  • Skip it if: you already have a card with straightforward cashback, or you buy vouchers at good discounts elsewhere.

  • What would fix it: letting you adjust cashback against the card bill at full value, with no extra fees.

Quick answers

Is it lifetime free?

Yes, no joining or annual fee. Other card charges and redemption charges can still apply.

Is the 5% credited to my card statement?

No statement credit has been confirmed. Kredit.Pe promotes redeeming it in its app.

Does Kredit.Pe charge for voucher redemption?

Yes. A redemption charge of about 1–2% on lower-discount gift cards, up to 4% on higher-discount ones, plus a 1% premium voucher charge on vouchers like Amazon and Flipkart. Check the final amount in the app before you redeem.

Is the cashback really worth 5%?

You can earn 5% on eligible spends, but what you get out is lower after charges, limits and the extra buying needed to redeem it.

Does the cashback expire?

Yes, one year from the end of the quarter in which you earned it.

Is there a monthly cashback cap?

Not confirmed. The Max card’s promotional details available so far don’t state one.
