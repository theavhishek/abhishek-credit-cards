Unity Bank Vibe vs Zero: the full review

Two RuPay credit cards from Unity Small Finance Bank, both backed by a fixed deposit, with 0% bank forex markup and rewards on UPI. Zero is free and pays up to 1% by category. Vibe costs ₹249 once, pays up to 1.5% on one of three plans, and adds 10 lounge visits a year.

The verdict: Vibe on Shopaholic, Zero as the free fallback

Vibe pays 0.5% more than Zero on shopping, electronics, jewellery and gift cards, and adds 10 lounge visits a year. Its ₹293.82 joining cost is mostly covered by the ₹250 Amazon Pay gift card. The real cost of either card is the FD you lock in: from ₹2,000 for Zero, ₹25,000 for Vibe.

  • Pick Vibe: you can park ₹25,000 or more in an FD, spend ₹15,000 in the first 45 days, and most of your spend fits one plan’s 1.5% categories.

  • Pick Zero: you want a no-fee secured RuPay card with 0% forex markup, a small FD and simple rewards.

  • Don’t pick either just for gift cards, ads or wallet loads. Unity can withhold rewards it sees as manufactured, circular or commercial. Check your own numbers in the calculator above.

Listed doesn’t mean allowed

Unity’s Cardholder Agreement bans buying to resell for gain and using the card commercially, for example as working capital. A personal purchase is fine; running ad spend or bulk gift-card resale through the card isn’t. Unity can close the card without notice for prohibited or commercial use.

Points are rounded per transaction

RP = ROUND(amount ÷ 100) × the RP rate, on each transaction. Under ₹100 earns nothing. A ₹1,000 shopping spend on Vibe Shopaholic earns 60 RP, worth ₹15. Splitting spends into small payments can earn less than the headline rate.

Catches in the documents

  • Zero replacement cards: KFS and MITC disagreeThe KFS allows up to two free direct replacements, the MITC one. Both then ask for 400 RP per request. Unity needs to say which applies.
  • Broad categories, strong misuse powersThe terms reward wallet loads, rent and advertising, but the Cardholder Agreement bars commercial use, resale and working capital. Repeated or self-funded transactions can lose their rewards or get the card restricted.
  • Still to confirm when you applyFD tenures on offer, how the physical card is issued, and whether UPI works from day one. The documents don’t settle these.

Quick answers

Is Vibe worth the ₹249 fee?

Usually. The ₹250 Amazon Pay gift card on ₹15,000 spend in 45 days covers most of the ₹293.82 cost with GST, and Vibe then earns 0.5% more than Zero on its plan’s categories. There’s no annual fee.

Which Vibe plan should I pick?

Shopaholic for clothes, department stores, electronics, jewellery and gift cards. Budget Balancer for rent, wallet loads, grocery, utilities and medical. Wanderer for flights, hotels and travel. You can change plans only every two statements.

How big an FD do I need?

At least ₹2,000 for Zero and ₹25,000 for Vibe. The credit limit is up to 90% of the FD principal, and closing the card liquidates the FD.

Do UPI payments earn rewards?

Yes, at the category rate, on both cards. The exception is credit card UPI payments of ₹2,000 or less at small offline merchants, which earn nothing.

Is there a cap on reward points?

No overall cap is stated for either card. The Cardholder Agreement does let Unity add a cap later, and it can withhold rewards on transactions it sees as manufactured or commercial.

Are these cards good abroad?

Both charge 0% bank forex markup, but international spends earn no rewards. Vibe adds 2 international lounge visits a year.
