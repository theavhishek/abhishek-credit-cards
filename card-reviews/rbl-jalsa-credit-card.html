The verdict: a simple app-cashback card with tight caps

5% on Swiggy, Zomato, Blinkit, Zepto, Flipkart and four more is a good rate, but it stops at ₹50 an order and ₹500 a month, you have to claim it every month, and nothing else on the card earns. About ₹2,000 a month on these apps covers the ₹1,179 fee.

  • Best fit: ₹4,000 to ₹10,000 a month of food, quick-commerce and fashion orders, mostly under ₹1,000 each.

  • Skip it if: you buy big-ticket items (a ₹5,000 order earns ₹50, so 1%), or want rewards on UPI, bills, fuel or everyday spends.

  • Set a reminder for the 3rd: that’s when each month’s claim banner opens in the MyBank app. Check your numbers in the calculator above.

Claiming the cashback

  • 3rd of the month: the claim banner opensYou get an email when you’re eligible. Open the MyBank app, then Offers & Rewards, then My Exclusive Offer.
  • Within 3 calendar months: confirm with OTPEach month has its own banner and needs its own OTP consent on your registered mobile number.
  • Within 15 days: credited to the cardIt shows on your next statement, and an email confirms the claim.

Cashback isn’t automatic

The terms say you must claim each calendar month’s cashback in the MyBank app. They give no rule for a month you don’t claim within the 3-month window, so don’t let one lapse.

Quick answers

Is the cashback credited automatically?

No. You claim each month in the MyBank app with an OTP, within 3 calendar months of the banner opening on the 3rd. It’s credited within 15 days of the claim.

Do I get 5% on every order?

Only up to ₹50 per order and ₹500 a month. A ₹600 Swiggy order earns ₹30; a ₹3,000 Myntra order earns ₹50.

Does anything else earn rewards?

No. The terms give cashback only on the nine apps. Other eligible spends count towards the ₹3 lakh milestone.

Is the annual fee waived on spends?

RBL’s MITC lists Jalsa at ₹999 for the first year and ₹999 from the second, with no spend-based waiver.

What is the welcome benefit really worth?

A ₹1,000 voucher. Redeeming it costs ₹99 + GST (₹116.82). You need a transaction within 30 days of getting the card.

What’s the most I can earn in a year?

₹6,000 of cashback, plus the ₹500 milestone voucher, plus ₹1,000 in the first year.
