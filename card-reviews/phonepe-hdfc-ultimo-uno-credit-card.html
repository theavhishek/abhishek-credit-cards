PhonePe HDFC Ultimo vs Uno: the full review

Two RuPay cards from HDFC Bank, sold only through PhonePe. Ultimo (₹999) pays 10% in reward points on recharges, bills and travel booked in the PhonePe app, 5% at seven online brands and 1% on UPI Scan & Pay. Uno (₹499) pays 2%, 1% and 1%. Every other spend earns nothing on both.

The verdict: Ultimo is worth it, Uno is hard to justify

Ultimo pays 10% on bills, recharges and travel paid in the PhonePe app, up to ₹1,000 a month. That one category alone covers its fee many times over. Uno pays 2%, which is ordinary, and nothing at all outside its three categories.

  • Take Ultimo: you pay electricity, mobile, broadband, DTH or other bills, or book flights, trains and buses, through PhonePe. ₹10,000 a month there fills the 10% cap.

  • Think twice about Uno at full price: the ₹589 fee (with GST) equals ₹29,450 of PhonePe-category spend at 2%, and the renewal is free only at ₹1 lakh a year.

  • Use it only where it pays. Swiping it at a shop, or paying any online merchant outside the list, earns zero points. Check your own numbers in the calculator above.

Applying, and what happens to your limit

  • Only through the PhonePe appOpen PhonePe, find the PhonePe HDFC credit card banner and fill in the form. There’s no HDFC website or branch route.
  • HDFC checks your credit scoreSome applicants also need to upload documents or do video KYC.
  • Already have an HDFC card? You can still applyUnless you have another HDFC card application from the last 60 days.
  • Your limit is shared, not addedHDFC’s example: with a ₹3 lakh limit on your existing card, the new card shares that ₹3 lakh. Under the floater card terms the combined limit is equal to or less than your existing limit.
  • No over-limit facilityThe MITC lists these two cards as having none.
  • Activate within 30 daysSet a PIN or make a transaction. After a 7-day grace period, the card is closed on day 37.
  • Link it to UPISet the card PIN first, then the UPI PIN in PhonePe: Manage Payments, Manage Card, Set PIN, then Proceed to Link.

Quick answers

Can I choose Ultimo instead of Uno?

No. HDFC decides the variant and your credit limit. In my experience a limit above ₹1.5 lakh usually gets Ultimo, and lower gets Uno, but it varies by customer.

What’s the most I can earn?

Ultimo: 2,000 points a month (1,000 at 10% + 500 at 5% + 500 on Scan & Pay), worth ₹2,000 as cashback. Uno: 1,500 points (500 + 500 + 500).

Do I earn on other spends?

No. Only PhonePe categories, the seven online brands and UPI Scan & Pay earn points. A swipe at a shop or another website earns nothing.

How do I turn points into money?

Redeem them as statement cashback in HDFC NetBanking: 1 point = ₹1, minimum 500 points, up to ₹7,500 a month, no fee. They expire after 2 years.

Can I get the fee waived?

Normally only the renewal fee: ₹2 lakh a year on Ultimo, ₹1 lakh on Uno, and the joining fee is charged within 60 days of issue.

Does either card have lounge access?

Only Ultimo: 2 domestic visits a quarter when you spent ₹75,000 in the quarter before, as a Gyftr voucher. Uno has none.

Is either card good abroad?

No. Both work internationally but charge a 3.5% foreign currency markup and earn no points there.
