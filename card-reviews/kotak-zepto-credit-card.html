Kotak × Zepto Credit Card: what’s confirmed so far

A Kotak Mahindra Bank card built for Zepto: 10% cashback on Zepto orders, 15% with Zepto Club, 5% on top partner brands, 1% on other spends and 0.5% on UPI, plus a free PVR INOX ticket every month you spend ₹5,000. The 5%, 1% and 0.5% share one ₹1,500 monthly cap.

The verdict: a Zepto card first, an everyday card second

If you order on Zepto every week, 10% back (15% with Club) plus a free movie ticket each month is a strong deal for ₹899 a year. Outside Zepto it’s ordinary: 1% on spends and 0.5% on UPI, sharing one ₹1,500 monthly cap with the 5% partner brands.

  • Get it: Zepto is where your groceries come from, and you’d use a free PVR INOX ticket most months.

  • Skip it: you want one card for everything, or you rarely use Zepto. 1% and 0.5% UPI won’t beat a good cashback card.

  • How I’d use it: Zepto orders, partner brands, and enough else to cross ₹5,000 a month for the ticket. Check your own spend in the calculator above.

The ₹1,500 cap

  • One cap, three rates5% partner brands, 1% other spends and 0.5% UPI all count towards the same ₹1,500 a month.
  • Partner brands alone can fill it₹30,000 at 5% is ₹1,500. After that, 1% and UPI spends earn nothing more that month.
  • Only 1%? You’d need ₹1.5 lakhAt 1% the cap is out of reach for most people, so it mostly matters if you spend big on partner brands.
  • Zepto’s 10% isn’t in this listThe cap is stated for the 5%, 1% and 0.5% categories. Whether Zepto cashback has a cap of its own isn’t stated yet.

How to apply

  1. Open the Zepto app (update it first).
  2. Find the Kotak × Zepto Credit Card. Early reports say it sits under Profile → Money Center.
  3. Check your eligibility and apply. Approval and credit limit are Kotak’s call.

It’s rolling out in batches, so if you can’t see it yet, check again in a few days.

Quick answers

Is it really 15% on Zepto?

Only with Zepto Club. The card gives 10% on eligible Zepto orders and Club adds 5%. Without Club, it’s 10%.

What is the monthly cashback cap?

₹1,500 a month, shared by the 5% partner-brand, 1% and 0.5% UPI cashback. Kotak hasn’t said whether the 10% Zepto cashback has its own cap.

How do I get the free movie ticket?

Spend ₹5,000 in a month and you get one PVR INOX ticket worth up to ₹250.

Is the card free?

The joining fee is ₹0 for a limited time. The annual fee is ₹899, waived when you spend ₹3 lakh in a year.

Where do I apply?

Only in the Zepto app. Find the Kotak × Zepto Credit Card, check eligibility and apply. It’s rolling out in batches.

Does UPI earn cashback?

Yes, 0.5% on eligible UPI spends, inside the ₹1,500 monthly cap.
