IDFC FIRST Hello Cashback: the full review

A secured RuPay credit card from IDFC FIRST Bank, backed by an FD from ₹10,000. It pays 3% online, 5% on online spend above ₹10,000, and 1% on offline, UPI in the IDFC FIRST app and essentials, with 0% forex markup. Online cashback stops at ₹1,000 a cycle and all cashback at ₹1,500.

The verdict: a good secured card, a side card for everyone else

For a secured card it pays well online, it rewards essentials most cards skip, and forex is 0%. But the 5% starts only after ₹10,000 online, online cashback stops at ₹1,000 a cycle, and your money sits in an FD.

  • Get it: you need a credit card without income proof or a credit history, you spend abroad, or you want 1% on bills and insurance that other cashback cards exclude.

  • Skip it: you already hold SBI Cashback or Swiggy HDFC for online spends. Those pay 5% from the first rupee.

  • Sweet spot: ₹24,000 online a cycle. That uses the full ₹1,000 online cap. Put the rest on another card. Check your own spend in the calculator above.

Paying early doesn’t reset cashback

Cashback-eligible spend in a cycle is capped at your credit limit on the statement date. Paying the card mid-cycle frees up limit, not cashback. The bank’s own example:

When cashback lands, and when you lose it

  • Credited after the due dateUsually one day after the payment due date, on eligible settled transactions.
  • Miss the minimum due, lose the cycleIf the minimum amount due isn’t paid by the due date, that cycle’s cashback is forfeited.
  • Refunds take it backRefunds and cancellations reverse their cashback. A negative balance can be debited in a later cycle.
  • Minimum due isn’t enoughIt keeps the cashback, but you pay interest. The June 2026 MITC shows 8.5% to 46.2% a year, set per cardholder. Check your own rate on your statement.

Quick answers

Do I get 5% on all online spends?

No. The first ₹10,000 of eligible online spend in a cycle earns 3%, and only the amount above it earns 5%. Online cashback stops at ₹1,000 a cycle, which you hit at ₹24,000.

What is the maximum cashback in a month?

₹1,500 a statement cycle across everything, with online cashback capped at ₹1,000 of that.

Does UPI on GPay or PhonePe earn cashback?

No. Only RuPay credit card UPI through the IDFC FIRST Bank app earns 1%. UPI through PhonePe, GPay, Paytm or other apps earns nothing.

How big an FD do I need?

At least ₹10,000, with extra FDs from ₹5,000. The credit limit is 100% of the linked FD, and the FD keeps earning interest.

Can I avoid the annual fee?

Spend ₹2 lakh in the card year and the ₹1,000 + GST renewal fee is reversed in full. ₹1 lakh to ₹2 lakh gets half back.

Is it good abroad?

Yes. The forex markup is 0% and eligible international spends can earn cashback. Pay in the local currency to avoid the merchant’s conversion charge.
