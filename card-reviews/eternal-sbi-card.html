Eternal SBI Credit Card: the full review

A ₹499 card promising 10% off across Zomato, Bistro and District, 5–10% on Blinkit, plus cashback on Uber, ixigo and more. The catch? Three different benefit caps and important exclusions. Here’s what actually matters before you request early access.

The verdict: excellent on paper, but not for every wallet

Eternal SBI Card is more interesting than its ₹499 fee suggests. It puts food delivery, quick commerce, movies and partner-platform cashback into one card. Yet the attractive 10% is an instant discount with a shared ₹2,000 monthly ceiling, not unlimited cashback on every order.

  • Best fit: you regularly order on Zomato, shop on Blinkit and use District, so the discounts fit spending you already do. The extra 5% partners may add useful savings.

  • Not a universal 10% card: most Blinkit categories get 5%, the partner-app discount pool is shared, and a broad list of spending categories may earn no base cashback.

  • The deciding test: estimate your savings without moving spending to more expensive orders. Check minimum values, excluded products and whether existing coupons can stack before applying.

On 15 October 2026, SBI Card and Eternal formally announced the card. You can see the card’s official product page (opens in a new tab); invitations to apply are due to start from 22 October 2026 in phases.

Two different reward mechanisms: instant discounts reduce what you pay at eligible checkouts; cashback is credited later to your card account. The 5% cashback and 1% base cashback are not additional instant discounts. Don’t assume the same transaction earns multiple categories simultaneously.

Blinkit’s 5% and 10% split is easy to miss

Buying groceries? That’s generally the 5% bucket, not 10%. SBI Card’s launch material names 10% for eligible Blinkit beauty and electronics, and 5% on other eligible Blinkit categories, subject to excluded items.

A ₹1,500 grocery basket at 5% gives an illustrative ₹75 discount. A ₹1,500 eligible electronics basket at 10% gives ₹150. Both amounts draw against the broader ₹2,000 monthly instant-discount ceiling alongside Zomato, Bistro and District.

Don’t assume the label on a product guarantees 10%

For mixed baskets, gift cards, beauty products sold under unusual categories, convenience fees and any payment-specific exclusions, trust the discount that appears in the app before placing the order. The final terms—not the headline—decide eligibility.

The useful takeaway: the card still has a grocery benefit. It is simply not the same 10% offer advertised for selected beauty and electronics.

Three reward pools, not one giant cashback rate

There are three separate buckets in the advertised structure. Understanding them prevents the most common mistake: adding every percentage together as though the same payment earns them all.

  • 1. Up to ₹2,000 in instant discounts per monthAcross eligible Zomato, Bistro, District and Blinkit purchases at their applicable 5% or 10% rates. This reduces checkout cost; it is not cashback paid later.
  • 2. Up to ₹1,000 in 5% partner cashback per monthFor eligible Uber, Urban Company, ixigo and Apollo 24|7 transactions. Reaching the full cap theoretically requires ₹20,000 of qualifying 5% partner spending.
  • 3. Up to ₹500 in 1% base cashback per monthFor otherwise eligible card purchases. Reaching that ceiling would require ₹50,000 of eligible base-rate spending; early-access terms state the ₹500 cap.

The theoretical combined ceilings add up to ₹3,500, not a guaranteed monthly payout: you would have to meet the independent conditions of each bucket, and an order may be excluded from another benefit. The District movie deal has its own reported ₹200 limit, but how it interacts with the ₹2,000 pool needs confirmation.

What about the 1% cashback exclusions?

Early benefit summaries list utilities, jewellery, rent, fuel, wallet loads, government payments, gaming, insurance, education and EMI transactions as excluded from general cashback. These are not a substitute for the card-specific MCC exclusion table; verify the complete list in SBI Card’s terms before making a large payment.

Cashback is described as an automatic card-statement credit. Check when it posts and how returns, cancellations and partial refunds affect eligibility.

Their raw 10% discounts would be ₹1,300 + ₹800 = ₹2,100, but the combined instant benefit is limited to ₹2,000. Additional eligible Blinkit spending cannot create an extra ₹2,000 allowance.

Fee break-even: ₹499 + 18% GST is ₹588.82. If you genuinely save 10% on existing purchases, about ₹491 in eligible spending every month for a year would cover that first-year fee. At a 5% rate, you need about ₹982 per month instead. Actual results depend on eligibility and how frequently you use the card.

The ₹990/month example intentionally keeps the benefit buckets separate and below their ceilings. It does not count movie BOGO, Zomato Gold, or any overlapping promotional discount. You can model your own eligible 10% spend with the calculator above.

₹499 fee: what you pay and what you get

The joining fee and renewal fee are each ₹499 plus applicable GST, which is ₹588.82 at an 18% tax rate. The six-month Zomato Gold membership is a joining benefit—not six months of discounts worth a fixed guaranteed cash amount.

  • First year: ₹499 + GSTLook at the actual savings from orders you would have placed anyway. A large 10% headline doesn’t justify paying for deliveries you did not need.
  • From year two: spend-based renewal waiverSBI Card’s launch release names ₹2 lakh of annual spending in the previous year as the waiver threshold. The product terms decide which transaction categories count.
  • Six months of Zomato GoldComplimentary membership attached to joining. If you already have Gold, check whether the benefit extends an existing subscription and when activation happens.
  • 1% fuel surcharge waiverAnnounced for the card, but the qualifying transaction band, maximum reversal and GST treatment should be read from the card-specific fee schedule.

For a card with a comparatively small joining fee, the real risk is not just the fee. It is trying to unlock the ₹2 lakh renewal waiver using transactions that either do not qualify or earn poor cashback.

Early access, application dates and RuPay UPI

15 October: SBI Card and Eternal announce the launch, and customers can request early access through the Zomato, Blinkit and District apps. 22 October 2026: application invitations are scheduled to start going out in phases. Requesting access is not the same as receiving an approved card.

The initial in-app campaign mentions only 5,000 cards. That is a limited-release message, not evidence that later phases are cancelled or that an early-access request guarantees approval. You can also check the official SBI Card Eternal product page (opens in a new tab) for newly published application and benefit information.

RuPay versus Mastercard: choose for how you pay

The launch confirms both networks. RuPay offers the possibility of linking the card for eligible merchant UPI payments under SBI Card and NPCI rules; Mastercard does not offer RuPay-credit-card-on-UPI functionality. Do not assume every QR payment earns 1% cashback—merchant classification and the Eternal card’s reward exclusions still matter.

Approval, credit limit, eligible serviceable locations and required documents remain subject to SBI Card’s application rules. Avoid third-party forms claiming guaranteed early approval.

The fine print I would check before applying

The broad benefits are official, but bank-issued product T&Cs can add exclusions that change how often you actually receive them. These are the important checks—not reasons to dismiss the card outright.

  • Stacking with app couponsCan a Zomato, Blinkit or District coupon be used together with this card’s instant discount? Is the discount applied before or after taxes, delivery fees and service fees?
  • Product and merchant exclusionsGift cards, electronics subcategories, mixed Blinkit carts, nonstandard District bookings and any restricted merchant category codes.
  • Monthly cap resetWhether each cap runs by calendar month or statement cycle; check the exact period before planning large purchases.
  • Movie ticket benefitHow the ₹200 BOGO saving is calculated, which shows qualify, whether fees count and whether the benefit shares the wider ₹2,000 discount cap.
  • Base rewards and UPIDetailed excluded MCCs, how eligible RuPay UPI merchant payments are classified, posting timing and what happens when purchases are refunded.
  • Annual-fee waiver and general accessWhich spending categories count toward ₹2 lakh, and when applications extend beyond the initial early-access group.

Until the complete card-specific terms are accessible and checked, the website reports launch benefits as announced and identifies separate early-access claims explicitly.

Questions worth answering before you apply

Has the Eternal SBI Card launched? Can I apply today?

It was formally announced on 15 October 2026, and early-access requests are open in the Zomato, Blinkit and District apps. Applications are planned to open in phases from 22 October 2026. A request or invitation does not guarantee approval.

Is the 10% discount on every Blinkit item?

No. The issuer highlights 10% on eligible beauty and electronics and 5% on other eligible Blinkit categories, including groceries. Some items are excluded. Mixed baskets and exact product categorisation should be checked at checkout.

Does each app get a separate ₹2,000 discount cap?

No. The ₹2,000 is a shared monthly instant-discount ceiling across eligible Eternal-partner app transactions. It is not ₹2,000 each for Zomato, Blinkit and District.

What is the difference between 5% partner cashback and 10% instant discount?

Instant discounts reduce the eligible order’s checkout amount. The 5% partner cashback on Uber, ixigo, Urban Company and Apollo 24|7 is credited later and has a separate ₹1,000 monthly cap. Check the posting and exclusion rules.

Does the Eternal SBI Card give 1% cashback on everything?

No. The early-access sheet specifies 1% on other eligible spends, capped at ₹500 per month. Major categories such as utilities, rent, wallets, insurance, government payments, education, gaming, jewellery, fuel and EMIs appear in exclusion summaries. Read the card-specific T&Cs for the precise MCC list.

Can I use Eternal SBI Card on UPI?

RuPay is one of the announced network choices. A RuPay version may be linked to supported UPI apps for eligible merchant payments subject to SBI Card and NPCI rules. Mastercard cannot be used for RuPay-credit-card-on-UPI. Confirm cashback eligibility separately.

What is the renewal fee waiver threshold?

₹2 lakh of eligible annual spends in the previous card year. The fee is ₹499 plus GST if the waiver is not met. Refer to the official card terms for qualifying and excluded spending categories.

Do I get free Zomato Gold?

The issuer announces a complimentary six-month Zomato Gold membership as a joining benefit. Check activation, validity and handling of an existing subscription when the detailed welcome terms are available.

Does District movie BOGO mean an unlimited free ticket?

No. Early-access details mention a monthly Buy 1 Get 1 ticket benefit up to ₹200. The terms of ticket value, convenience fees, eligible bookings and interaction with other discounts must still be checked.

Is the card lifetime free? Is the 5,000-card limit permanent?

No, the announced joining and renewal fees are ₹499 plus GST, with renewal waiver based on spending. The first 5,000 cards refer to the initial in-app early-access campaign, not a confirmed lifetime issuance limit.
