Eternal SBI Credit Card: benefits and early access

SBI Card and Eternal announced the card on 15 October 2026. It offers up to 10% instant discounts on Zomato, Blinkit and District, subject to a shared ₹2,000 monthly cap. Early-access requests are appearing in the Zomato app.

Is it worth considering?

It looks useful for regular Zomato, Blinkit and District spending, especially with a ₹499 + GST fee. But the ₹2,000 shared instant-discount cap and category restrictions matter more than the headline 10%.

  • Worth considering if you frequently use Zomato, Blinkit and District and can benefit from the applicable instant discounts each month.

  • Watch the limits: ordinary Blinkit groceries receive a reported 5%, not 10%, and the ₹2,000 monthly instant-discount allowance is shared across eligible partner-app transactions.

  • Before applying, check the final product terms for excluded items, minimum orders, offer stacking, partner cashback credit and qualifying spends for the renewal-fee waiver.

Instant discounts and post-transaction cashback have separate monthly caps. Do not assume an order earns both or that two instant offers will stack unless the card terms explicitly allow it. The launch report also mentions 10% at Bistro and a fuel-surcharge waiver; verify their conditions before relying on them.

The Blinkit split: 5% versus 10%

The earlier teaser suggested groceries were excluded from the headline 10%. The 15 October launch information is more specific: 5% on other eligible Blinkit purchases, including groceries, and 10% on beauty and electronics. Some items are excluded.

Check mixed baskets and exclusions

The final terms still need to clarify which products count as beauty or electronics, how a mixed-category cart is discounted, and whether gift cards, wallet products or other restricted items qualify.

What still needs confirming

The product has been announced and early-access invitations are visible, but the complete bank-issued cardholder terms and detailed offer rules are not all available in the sources checked.

  • Per-order limits and exclusionsMinimum order values, excluded items, transactions, MCCs and any individual discount ceilings.
  • Offer stackingWhether Zomato, Blinkit and District discounts combine with other promotions or any cashback on the same transaction.
  • Movie offer mechanicsEligible ticket prices, ₹200 monthly ceiling treatment, number of uses and any booking restrictions.
  • Cashback postingSettlement timing, exclusions and whether refunds affect the 5% or 1% monthly limits.
  • Annual-spend waiver definitionQualifying transaction types and exact threshold calculation for the ₹2 lakh renewal waiver.
  • Early access and wider rolloutHow the first-5,000-card invitation is allocated, underwriting eligibility, and timing of general application access.

Questions

Can I request the Eternal SBI Card now?

Early-access requests are appearing within Zomato. Check the Zomato app for the Eternal SBI Credit Card invitation; its initial rollout mentions 5,000 cards. Launch reporting says digital application is also intended through Eternal partner apps, subject to availability and approval. Avoid unofficial application links.

Is the 10% discount capped?

Yes. The combined instant discount across the partner ecosystem is capped at ₹2,000 per month. Per-order restrictions, stacking rules and exclusions still need review.

Do Blinkit groceries get a discount?

The launch announcement states 5% on other eligible Blinkit purchases, including groceries, versus 10% on eligible beauty and electronics. Certain items are excluded.

Are the cashback limits separate from the instant-discount cap?

The 5% cashback on Uber, Urban Company, ixigo and Apollo 24|7 has a shared ₹1,000 monthly limit. Zomato’s early-access material separately reports 1% on other eligible spends capped at ₹500/month. These are not the same as the ₹2,000 instant-discount cap.

What is Eternal?

Eternal Limited is the company behind Zomato, Blinkit and District.
