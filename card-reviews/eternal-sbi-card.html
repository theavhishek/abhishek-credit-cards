Eternal SBI Credit Card preview

SBI Card is reportedly preparing a co-branded card with Eternal, the company behind Zomato, Blinkit and District. Early material promises 10% off on all three. Fees, caps and the launch date aren’t confirmed yet.

Should you wait for it?

Worth watching, too early to recommend. 10% off on Zomato and District is strong on paper, but without caps and final fees there’s no way to know what the card is actually worth.

  • Wait for it if you order on Zomato or book on District every month. At the rumoured fee, about ₹500 a month of eligible spend would pay for the card.

  • Look elsewhere if most of your Blinkit spend is groceries. The reported 10% excludes them.

  • Before you apply, check the monthly cap and minimum order in SBI Card’s terms. Those two numbers decide the card’s real value.

The biggest catch: Blinkit groceries are excluded

Blinkit is one of the three partner apps, but the reported 10% instant discount doesn’t apply to groceries, which is what most people order there. Non-grocery items may qualify.

Not clear yet

Which product categories count as non-grocery, whether gift cards qualify, and how SBI Card treats a mixed order with groceries and other items.

What’s still unknown

SBI Card hasn’t disclosed these details yet:

  • Discount capsThe maximum discount per transaction and per month. This decides most of the card’s value.
  • Minimum order and eligible merchantsA high minimum would rule out smaller orders.
  • Gift cards and vouchersWhether buying Zomato or Blinkit gift cards earns the discount.
  • 5% valueback rulesWhich merchants qualify, and how valueback is credited and redeemed.
  • Movie offer limitsThe maximum discount per ticket and any conditions on the Buy 1 Get 1 offer.
  • Fees and fee waiverThe confirmed joining fee, renewal fee and the spend needed to waive it.
  • Network, eligibility and how to applyVisa, Mastercard or RuPay, income criteria and the application process.
  • Launch dateApplications aren’t open yet.

Questions

Can I apply for the Eternal SBI Card now?

Not yet. SBI Card hasn’t opened applications or published the card’s terms. Avoid unofficial links that claim to take applications.

Is the 10% discount capped?

Not known. No cap has been published. Co-branded discount cards usually have one, so treat the uncapped numbers on this page as the most you could save.

Do Blinkit groceries get 10% off?

No. The reported Blinkit discount covers non-grocery purchases only.

What is Eternal?

Eternal Limited is the parent company of Zomato, Blinkit and District.
