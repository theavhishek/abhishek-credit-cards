The verdict

The HSBC Live+ Credit Card is one of the more compelling cashback cards in India for people who regularly spend on groceries, restaurants, food delivery, eligible shopping and utility bills. Its main attraction is 10% cashback across these categories, subject to a combined ₹1,200 monthly cap, alongside unlimited 1.5% cashback on most other eligible domestic spending.

Following its July 2026 revision, the card offers a broader accelerated cashback programme, Visa Infinite privileges and international lounge access. But not every change is an improvement: domestic lounge access has been reduced, certain transactions no longer earn cashback, and the headline shopping benefit excludes major platforms such as Amazon and Flipkart.

Bottom line: HSBC Live+ works best as a dedicated everyday-spending card, not necessarily as your only credit card. Its value depends on how consistently you can use the 10% categories without crossing the shared cap.

2. Cashback structure: How the rewards actually work

HSBC Live+ has two reward tiers.

10% accelerated cashback

The card offers 10% cashback on eligible transactions in five major categories:

  • Dining at restaurants and eligible food outlets
  • Food delivery
  • Groceries
  • Shopping at qualifying merchants
  • Utility payments under supported merchant category codes

These categories share one combined cashback cap of ₹1,200 per billing cycle.

This distinction matters. You do not receive separate ₹1,200 caps for groceries, food delivery, utilities and shopping.

For example, if you earn ₹500 cashback on groceries and ₹400 on dining, only ₹300 remains available under the accelerated cashback allowance for that billing cycle.

What spending is needed to maximise cashback?

At a 10% rate, ₹12,000 of qualifying accelerated spending generates ₹1,200 cashback.

After the cap is reached, additional spending in the accelerated categories does not generate further accelerated cashback. Do not assume the excess automatically earns 1.5%; HSBC's published calculation caps the cashback available for those transactions.

The ideal monthly accelerated spending target is around ₹12,000. Beyond that, evaluate whether another card can deliver better returns on the remaining transactions.

1.5% cashback on other eligible spending

HSBC provides unlimited 1.5% cashback on most other eligible purchases that do not qualify for the accelerated programme.

Typical examples can include qualifying hotel bookings, travel purchases, entertainment and general retail transactions that are not subject to an exclusion.

3. Merchant category codes: The most important fine print

HSBC determines regular cashback eligibility primarily through the Merchant Category Code (MCC) assigned to each transaction.

An MCC is a four-digit classification associated with a merchant. Two businesses selling similar products may use different MCCs and consequently earn different cashback rates.

Why this matters: The merchant's name or the products purchased do not, by themselves, guarantee 10% cashback. The posted MCC and any merchant-specific exclusions ultimately determine eligibility.

Grocery and quick-commerce transactions

HSBC Live+ is particularly attractive for eligible grocery purchases at supermarkets and grocery-focused merchants, including qualifying online grocery orders.

Platforms such as Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart and other quick-commerce services can be relevant, but cashback should never be promised solely on the basis of the app name.

An order coded as an eligible grocery MCC can qualify for 10%. A differently coded order may receive a lower rate or no cashback.

Utility bills

The 2026 revision expanded accelerated cashback to qualifying utility transactions, including MCC 4900 and certain telecommunications classifications.

This can make the card useful for electricity bills, water bills and eligible telecom payments. However, the route used for payment can affect the MCC.

A direct merchant payment, payment aggregator and third-party bill payment platform may not all classify a transaction identically.

Recommendation: Before making a large bill payment solely for rewards, verify the transaction's MCC or cashback treatment with HSBC.

4. Shopping cashback: A major exception

The 2026 expansion of the 10% category to shopping looks attractive, but this is where readers need to pay particular attention.

HSBC explicitly excludes shopping transactions at Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra from the standard 10% category. These merchants ordinarily earn 1.5% cashback on otherwise eligible spending.

There is, however, a temporary Myntra promotion.

Limited-time Myntra benefit

From 31 July to 31 October 2026, qualifying transactions on the Myntra website and app can earn 10% cashback.

The cashback remains part of the same ₹1,200 billing-cycle cap. It is not an additional allowance. Under the published offer, Myntra returns to its ordinary 1.5% treatment after 31 October 2026.

This distinction should influence your card selection:

  • Eligible offline retail: potentially 10%.
  • Eligible specialty online retailers: potentially 10%, depending on MCC and exceptions.
  • Amazon and Flipkart: generally 1.5% on eligible purchases.
  • Myntra: temporary 10% promotion through 31 October 2026.

For someone making substantial purchases on Amazon and Flipkart, a dedicated online cashback card may be more rewarding than HSBC Live+.

Your effective cashback rate drops to 4.5% on ₹30,000 of total spending.

This illustrates the card's core limitation: once the accelerated cap is exhausted, continuing to channel every purchase onto HSBC Live+ may not be optimal.

7. Lounge access: Important 2026 changes

HSBC Live+ now provides three complimentary airport lounge visits annually:

Lounge typeEntitlement
Domestic2 visits per calendar year
International1 visit per calendar year

Domestic lounge access

The domestic entitlement is limited to one complimentary visit during each six-month calendar period.

Effectively, this means one visit in January–June and one in July–December.

The earlier benefit of four domestic visits annually has been reduced to two. This is a meaningful cut for frequent domestic travellers.

Access is available only at participating lounges and is subject to capacity, validation and programme rules. Add-on cardholders have separate domestic lounge eligibility under the published terms.

International lounge access

The international lounge benefit became effective in September 2026 and covers one complimentary visit per calendar year at participating lounges outside India.

It is not simply an unrestricted airport lounge swipe.

Cardholders must use the dedicated Live+ partner platform to validate their card and generate lounge access credentials. The booking must be made at least 23 hours before the intended visit.

The redemption platform is hsbcliveplus.thriwe.com. Eligible cardholders are required to follow the platform's validation process, including a refundable ₹25 card-verification amount.

The international lounge and Global eSIM partner programme is currently stated to run until 31 August 2027.

8. Forex charges and international transactions

HSBC reduced the standard forex markup on Live+ to 1.99%.

But there is an important trade-off: under the revised programme, international transactions no longer earn the regular 1.5% cashback.

The usual 1.99% forex markup also attracts applicable taxes and potentially other conversion-related charges.

Temporary zero-forex offer

HSBC is currently running a promotional 0% forex markup offer from 15 August to 31 October 2026.

The offer covers qualifying foreign-currency POS and online transactions on eligible HSBC credit cards, including Live+. Corporate credit cards and ATM withdrawals are excluded.

The waiver does not automatically eliminate every possible charge, including cross-border network charges and regulatory levies where applicable.

The transaction posting date is important: a purchase initiated during the offer window but posted after it ends may not qualify.

Verdict on international spending: HSBC Live+ can be useful during the temporary forex promotion. After it expires, the standard 1.99% markup and lack of international cashback make a dedicated low-forex card worth considering.

9. Visa Infinite lifestyle benefits

The 2026 revision brings Visa Infinite positioning and additional travel and lifestyle privileges.

Selected advertised benefits include:

  • Hotel offers with participating ITC and IHG properties
  • Visa Concierge services
  • Avis car-rental privileges
  • Selected premium dining and retail discounts
  • Airport Meet & Assist benefits subject to qualifying international spending
  • Offers across lifestyle and wellness partners

For example, HSBC advertises Meet & Assist eligibility linked to spending more than USD 1,000 abroad during the preceding 12 months.

These are partner benefits, not guaranteed monetary rewards. Availability, booking channels, eligibility and offer validity matter.

A Visa Infinite logo does not automatically make this card comparable to every other premium Visa Infinite credit card.

10. The Live+ Reserve dining programme

Introduced in August 2026, The Live+ Reserve is a curated dining and lifestyle programme powered by DineWithTimesPrime.

The programme provides access to selected restaurants, curated menus, complimentary beverages or desserts, and participating lifestyle-brand offers.

The published programme includes a dedicated booking and redemption portal, with benefits subject to restaurant availability and partner restrictions.

A small ₹1 verification or redemption payment may be required, depending on the benefit.

This is a useful addition for customers who actively dine at participating premium restaurants. For those who rarely use such venues, the practical value may be limited.

It is best viewed as a supplementary lifestyle perk rather than a replacement for the card's cashback programme.

11. Fuel offer: Separate from normal cashback

HSBC Live+ does not provide ordinary fuel cashback under its standard rewards programme.

However, a promotional contactless fuel benefit is currently available.

The offer provides ₹250 cashback after ₹10,000 of eligible contactless fuel spending in a calendar quarter.

Important conditions include:

  • Eligible domestic fuel purchases only
  • Payment must be made through contactless card transactions
  • Each qualifying transaction must be between ₹1,000 and ₹5,000
  • Supported fuel MCCs include 5541, 5542 and 5983
  • The published Live+ offer runs until 31 December 2026
  • Cashback is credited subject to the quarterly fulfilment terms

At exactly ₹10,000 of qualifying spending, ₹250 represents a 2.5% promotional return before considering applicable fuel surcharges and other costs.

This is not equivalent to a permanent 2.5% fuel reward programme.

12. Movie and entertainment offers

HSBC Live+ is eligible for selected entertainment promotions through District and BookMyShow.

District by Zomato

Under the published promotion running from August to October 2026, eligible Live+ cardholders can receive a buy-one-get-one movie-ticket discount of up to ₹200 on the second ticket using the qualifying offer code.

The offer is subject to participating dates, booking limits and other platform conditions.

BookMyShow

Eligible HSBC cards can receive a Saturday movie-ticket buy-one-get-one offer with a discount of up to ₹250.

It is limited to the first 2,000 eligible cardholders booking on a Saturday and normally to one use per card per calendar month.

HSBC also advertises 10% off eligible live events, subject to a monthly limit and availability restrictions.

These offers are currently advertised through 31 October 2026 and should not be assumed to continue indefinitely.

13. Welcome benefits for new cardholders

HSBC advertises several onboarding benefits, but their eligibility conditions differ.

₹1,000 introductory cashback

New eligible Live+ cardholders applying during the specified promotional period can receive ₹1,000 cashback after downloading or logging into the HSBC India Mobile Banking app and completing at least ₹25,000 of qualifying transactions within 30 days of card issuance.

The offer currently applies to eligible applications from 26 July to 31 December 2026.

Other introductory promotions

Additional advertised offers include:

  • ₹750 voucher benefit linked to qualifying card activation
  • ₹250 Amazon eGift Voucher for eligible online applications with Video KYC
  • A separate ₹1,000 promotion linked to qualifying applications using Account Aggregator income documentation

Important: Do not assume all introductory benefits can be combined. HSBC's ₹1,000 spending-based welcome cashback terms prohibit combining it with another welcome or introductory offer.

Check the precise offer under which the card is issued before assigning monetary value to welcome benefits.

14. Annual fees and fee waiver

The standard fee is ₹999 plus applicable GST for joining and annual renewal.

HSBC states that the annual fee, including applicable GST, will be fully reversed when eligible spending across primary and add-on cards exceeds ₹2,00,000 during the relevant card anniversary year.

The word exceeds is important: do not assume spending exactly ₹2,00,000 satisfies a strictly greater-than requirement.

The joining fee is charged separately and should not be confused with the annual renewal waiver.

At a practical level, ₹2 lakh per year represents an average of about ₹16,667 in monthly spending.

It is achievable for some households, but manufacturing unnecessary spending purely to avoid a ₹999 annual fee makes little financial sense.

HSBC Live+ vs SBI Cashback

SBI Cashback is particularly relevant if your spending is concentrated on online retail outside HSBC's 10% merchant eligibility.

Under SBI Card's revised April 2026 structure, eligible online transactions earn 5%, capped at ₹2,000 per statement cycle, while eligible offline purchases earn 1%, separately capped at ₹2,000.

HSBC Live+ is generally more attractive for qualifying grocery purchases, eligible dining and other accelerated categories until the ₹1,200 cap is exhausted.

These cards can complement each other rather than serve as direct replacements.

HSBC Live+ vs HDFC Swiggy

The HDFC Swiggy card concentrates its best returns on eligible Swiggy purchases and also offers cashback on selected online shopping.

HSBC Live+ provides broader qualifying opportunities across groceries, restaurants, utilities and shopping, subject to MCC and merchant restrictions.

A frequent Swiggy customer may prefer the HDFC card for spending beyond HSBC's shared cashback limit, while using HSBC for other eligible merchants.

HSBC Live+ vs Amazon Pay ICICI

Amazon Pay ICICI is especially relevant for purchases on Amazon, where HSBC ordinarily earns only 1.5%.

Amazon Pay ICICI offers 5% on eligible Amazon shopping for Prime members and 3% for non-Prime members, subject to product exclusions and its terms.

It is also lifetime free under its standard pricing.

For a household primarily shopping on Amazon, Amazon Pay ICICI may be the more sensible first choice.

16. Who should get HSBC Live+?

Best suited for

Everyday household spenders: Customers spending approximately ₹6,000–₹12,000 a month on eligible groceries, food delivery, dining, shopping and utilities.

People using multiple credit cards: Cardholders who want to use HSBC Live+ specifically for its strongest categories and other cards after reaching the monthly cap.

Customers preferring direct cashback: There are no reward points, transfer partners or redemption ratios to optimise. Eligible cashback is credited to the card account.

Occasional travellers and diners: Customers who can use the limited lounge visits and selected Visa Infinite or dining privileges.

Who should avoid it?

Heavy Amazon and Flipkart shoppers: The normal 1.5% return at these merchants may be inferior to dedicated online shopping cashback cards.

Frequent domestic flyers: Two domestic lounge visits annually may be insufficient.

Customers seeking rewards on insurance, rent or education: Many transactions in these categories earn zero cashback.

People who routinely carry outstanding balances: HSBC's published finance charges can easily exceed the cashback earned. Cashback optimisation only makes sense when the outstanding bill is repaid in full.

People needing credit-card UPI payments: This Visa card does not provide the RuPay credit-card-on-UPI functionality.

17. Advantages and drawbacks

Advantages

  • High 10% cashback rate on useful everyday spending categories
  • Expanded category coverage after the July 2026 changes
  • Unlimited 1.5% cashback on most other eligible transactions
  • Automatic cashback credit without reward redemption
  • Annual fee waiver available at a relatively accessible spending threshold
  • Visa Infinite lifestyle benefits
  • Domestic and international lounge entitlements
  • Selected movie, dining and travel promotions

Drawbacks

  • One combined ₹1,200 cap across all accelerated categories
  • Major e-commerce merchant exclusions from the regular 10% rate
  • Extensive zero-cashback MCC exclusions
  • Domestic lounge visits reduced compared with the previous structure
  • No standard cashback on international transactions
  • Regular forex markup of 1.99% outside temporary promotions
  • Cashback posting may take up to 45 days after the statement date
  • Some lifestyle benefits require separate platforms, booking steps and eligibility checks
  • Card applications are limited to HSBC's specified serviceable cities and eligibility criteria

18. Final verdict: Is HSBC Live+ worth applying for in 2026?

HSBC Live+ remains a strong everyday cashback card after its 2026 restructuring, particularly for customers whose spending patterns naturally match the accelerated reward categories.

The combination of 10% cashback on eligible groceries, dining, food delivery, shopping and utilities makes it relatively easy to generate meaningful savings without chasing complex points programmes.

However, the shared ₹1,200 cap is the central limitation. The card's advertised reward rate is not its effective reward rate on every rupee spent.

The 2026 changes have also introduced a mixed package of improvements and cuts. A higher cashback cap, expanded categories and international lounge privileges add value, while reduced domestic lounge access, additional exclusions and the removal of international cashback reduce value for other users.

My assessment: HSBC Live+ is worth considering if you can consistently generate meaningful cashback on eligible everyday purchases and pay your statement in full. It makes less sense as an all-purpose card for unrestricted shopping, travel or excluded payment categories.

The smartest approach is to use it where the 10% cashback genuinely applies, keep track of the ₹1,200 cap and redirect remaining spending to cards with stronger returns in those categories.

Frequently asked questions

Is HSBC Live+ lifetime free?

No. It normally charges ₹999 plus GST as a joining fee and annual fee. The renewal fee can be reversed after meeting the eligible annual spending threshold.

What is the maximum cashback per month?

The 10% accelerated cashback is capped at ₹1,200 per billing cycle. The separate 1.5% cashback on most other eligible transactions is advertised as unlimited.

Does HSBC Live+ give 10% cashback on Amazon and Flipkart?

No. HSBC explicitly excludes these merchants from the standard 10% shopping category. Otherwise eligible transactions ordinarily earn 1.5%.

Does HSBC Live+ offer 10% cashback on Myntra?

Yes, for qualifying Myntra purchases during the limited promotion ending 31 October 2026. The same ₹1,200 accelerated cashback cap applies.

Does the card give 10% cashback on electricity bills?

Eligible utility transactions under supported MCCs, including MCC 4900, qualify. Payment gateways and merchant classifications may affect eligibility.

Does it give 10% cashback on Blinkit or Zepto?

It can on eligible grocery-classified transactions, but cashback depends on the merchant's actual MCC and applicable exclusions.

Does it earn cashback on insurance payments?

No. Insurance transactions under the excluded MCCs do not earn standard cashback.

How long does cashback take to arrive?

HSBC states that qualifying cashback is credited to the credit card account within 45 days of the statement date.

Are the lounge visits spend-based?

The current published lounge terms specify annual entitlements and usage restrictions without listing a minimum-spend requirement for those complimentary visits. Participation, capacity and programme conditions still apply.

Is HSBC Live+ good for international spending?

There is a temporary zero-forex-markup promotion through 31 October 2026. The normal forex markup is 1.99%, and the regular 1.5% cashback no longer applies to international transactions.

Can add-on cards earn additional cashback?

Eligible transactions on add-on cards can contribute to the card account's rewards, but there is no separate ₹1,200 accelerated cashback allowance for each add-on card. Some lounge and partner entitlements do provide separate add-on eligibility under their individual terms.
