HSBC Live+ Credit Card review

10% cashback on everyday categories sounds generous. The catch is one ₹1,200 cap shared across all of them, every billing cycle. Here is when that works for you, and when it doesn’t.

Verdict

HSBC Live+ is a strong everyday cashback card if you regularly spend on eligible groceries, dining, food delivery, shopping and utilities. Its biggest limitation is the shared ₹1,200 cashback cap per billing cycle.

Use it for qualifying 10% purchases and switch to another card after reaching the cap.

  • Get it if you naturally spend ₹6,000–₹12,000 monthly in eligible 10% categories and pay the bill in full.

  • Skip it if you mostly shop on Amazon or Flipkart, want RuPay credit-card UPI, or often pay excluded categories.

  • Optimise it by moving to a better-suited second card once the accelerated cap is used.

₹12,000 in eligible 10% purchases = ₹1,200 cashback. The cap is combined, not ₹1,200 for each category. Spending above the accelerated cap does not automatically earn 1.5%. Cashback is credited directly to the account within 45 days of the statement date.

Amazon and Flipkart do not get the standard 10% shopping rate; otherwise eligible purchases ordinarily earn 1.5%. Myntra earns a promotional 10% through 31 October 2026, subject to the same ₹1,200 cap.

0% cashback

Fuel, insurance, rent, government payments, many education payments, wallet loads, jewellery, hospital MCC 8062, advertising MCC 7311 and toll MCC 4784.

Blinkit, Zepto, bill payments and gift cards aren’t guaranteed 10% merely because of the merchant name. Check the posted MCC. The examples above are not a complete MCC list.

Forex and international purchases

Normal forex markup is 1.99% plus applicable taxes; international transactions no longer earn regular 1.5% cashback. A temporary zero-forex offer ends 31 October 2026 for qualifying purchases. Other fees and posting restrictions may still apply.

Questions

Is the ₹1,200 cap separate for groceries and dining?

No. All 10% categories share a single ₹1,200 cap each billing cycle.

Does Blinkit or Zepto get 10%?

Only when the transaction has an eligible MCC and no exclusion applies.

Can I use this as a RuPay card on UPI?

No, HSBC Live+ is on Visa.
