Swiggy HDFC Ornge vs BLCK: the full review

HDFC Bank has split the Swiggy card in two. Ornge (₹500) pays 5% on Swiggy and select online shopping, sharing one ₹1,500 monthly cap. BLCK (₹1,000) pays 10% on Swiggy and 5% online, each with its own ₹1,500 cap. Both pay 1% on other spends, up to ₹1,000.

The verdict: BLCK if you qualify, Ornge if you don’t

BLCK earns at least as much as Ornge on every rupee and has ₹1,500 more cap room each month. The extra ₹590 a year in fees is covered by about ₹1,000 a month on Swiggy. The catch is eligibility: BLCK asks for ₹1 lakh a month in salary, Ornge ₹20,000.

  • Pick BLCK: you order on Swiggy every week, also shop on Amazon or Flipkart, and spend ₹2 lakh a year on the card so the renewal is free.

  • Pick Ornge: you don’t meet BLCK’s income bar, spend under ₹1,000 a month on Swiggy, or can get Ornge lifetime free.

  • Keep Swiggy orders at ₹249 or more. Smaller orders earn nothing. Check your own numbers in the calculator above.

Old Swiggy card, upgrades and free offers

  • The old Swiggy HDFC card is being phased outHolders get SMS and email at least 30 days before. If you don’t pick a variant, the card moves to Ornge.
  • Migrated Ornge cards are lifetime freePer HDFC’s migration FAQ (29 October 2026). The same FAQ also says the joining fee is charged within 120 days and the renewal date resets, so check your statement.
  • New number, old card for 60 daysThe old card stops on your first new-card transaction, after 60 days, or at its expiry. Autopay, mandates and card controls don’t move over.
  • Migration welcome couponSwiggy One for 1 year, sent within 20 days of activation. Redeem within a year of card issue.
  • Old-card cashback queriesUp to one month from the new card’s issue date. Cashback around migration posts within 5 business days.
  • Switching between Ornge and BLCKYour card must be at least 6 months old. Ornge to BLCK needs a ₹1.5 lakh credit limit, and HDFC’s internal checks.
  • Ornge lifetime freeFor applications through the Swiggy app from 1 July 2026. Also for staff of select employers applying on HDFC’s own channels from 1 April 2026; they see the ₹500 fee while applying, and it’s removed within 90 days.
  • Ornge first year free has endedIt covered applications from 15 April to 10 September 2026.
  • Swiggy app offers stay on that numberOffers shown in the Swiggy app work only with the mobile number on that Swiggy account.

Quick answers

Is BLCK always better than Ornge?

On cashback, yes: it pays the same or more on every spend. After fees, BLCK wins once you spend about ₹1,000 a month on Swiggy, unless your Ornge is lifetime free.

Is the cashback credited automatically?

Yes. Each billing cycle’s cashback is adjusted against your card outstanding. There’s nothing to claim.

What’s the most I can earn?

Ornge: ₹2,500 a billing cycle (₹1,500 at 5% + ₹1,000 at 1%). BLCK: ₹4,000 (₹1,500 at 10% + ₹1,500 at 5% + ₹1,000 at 1%).

Can I get the fee waived?

Ornge renewal: spend ₹1.5 lakh a year. BLCK: ₹75,000 in the first 90 days waives the joining fee, ₹2 lakh a year waives renewal. Ornge is lifetime free through the Swiggy app.

What happens to my old Swiggy HDFC card?

It’s being phased out. Unless you choose BLCK, it moves to a lifetime-free Ornge card with a new number, and you get a 1-year Swiggy One coupon.

Is either card good abroad?

No. Both charge a 3.5% foreign currency markup and have no lounge access listed.
