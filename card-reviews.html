HSBC Live+ Credit Card

A Reviewed by Abhishek MeenaIndependent card analysis

A strong lifestyle cashback card for people who consistently spend on dining, food delivery and groceries—but the accelerated return has a monthly ceiling.

Accelerated cashback
10%
Up to ₹1,200 monthly
Base cashback
1.5%
On most other eligible spends
Annual fee
₹999
Waived above ₹2 lakh yearly spend

Why it works

The accelerated categories make it easy to recover the annual fee if food and grocery spends are already part of your monthly routine.

Watch the cap

The 10% bucket is capped at ₹1,200 each month. Once it is exhausted, the headline return no longer tells the full story.

Best suited for

Cardholders who can concentrate eligible dining, food-delivery and grocery transactions on one card without forcing extra spending.

Quick verdict

Keep it for the accelerated lifestyle categories; pair it with another card for excluded categories and spends beyond the cap.

Card features and eligibility can change. Verify the latest terms with HSBC before applying.
