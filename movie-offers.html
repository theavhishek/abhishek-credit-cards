Questions people ask

Why does an offer not work on my card?

Most offers depend on the card variant, the network (Visa, Mastercard or RuPay), a monthly limit and sometimes the day or the app. Open the offer’s terms on the platform: they list the exact cards and limits. The platform applies the discount on the payment page, so the final check is there.

Do I need a promo code?

Usually not. On BookMyShow most bank offers apply when you pick the offer on the payment page and pay with the eligible card. If an offer needs a code, its terms say so.

Which ticket is free on Buy 1 Get 1?

With Buy 1 Get 1 offers the discount is usually on the second ticket, up to the cap in the terms. Convenience fees and taxes are often not covered.

How current is this list?

Each offer shows the date its source was read. “Terms checked” offers had their caps, limits and dates read from the official terms; “Also on BookMyShow” offers only show the platform’s headline so far. Banks change these often, so the official terms win if anything here differs.
