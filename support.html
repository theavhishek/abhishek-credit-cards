How to escalate a complaint

Go one step at a time and keep your complaint or reference number. Each bank below lists the contact for every step. If a bill payment hasn’t reached your card, check you used the official payment details for your bank.

  1. 1

    Customer care

    Call the helpline or email customer care first, and note the reference number they give you.

  2. 2

    Nodal officer

    Not sorted? Email the nodal officer with your reference number and what has happened so far.

  3. 3

    Principal nodal officer

    The bank’s most senior complaint contact. Write here if the nodal officer’s reply doesn’t fix it.

  4. 4

    RBI Ombudsman

    If the bank rejects your written complaint or doesn’t reply within 30 days, file it free on cms.rbi.org.in (opens in a new tab).
