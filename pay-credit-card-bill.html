Before you pay

Money sent to a wrong account is hard to get back.

  • Use only official details

    Never pay to a UPI ID, QR code or account number sent by call, SMS or WhatsApp.

  • Check the name before you confirm

    Your app shows who gets the money. If it isn’t the card’s bank or you, stop.

  • Leave time for the money to land

    NEFT and gateways can take 1 to 3 working days. Near the due date, use an instant method.

  • Keep the reference number

    Save the UTR or transaction ID until the payment shows on your card.

Before paying, verify the recipient details displayed by your UPI application. If the payee cannot be verified or appears incorrect, do not proceed. Use the bank’s official payment page or contact the bank for confirmation.

Paying through PhonePe, Google Pay or CRED?

Since July 2024, credit card bills in apps like these go through Bharat Connect (formerly BBPS), the RBI-backed bill payment network. Pick your bank under credit card bills and the app fetches your bill. If your card isn’t listed, its bank hasn’t joined on that app. Pay with the bank’s own app, net banking or the transfer details above instead.

Banks marked Live on BBPS were in the Bharat Connect biller list on 14 October 2026. Pick the exact name shown on the bank’s card. Fintech cards such as the OneCard “One” cards from CSB, Indian Bank, BOBCARD and SIB have biller names of their own. American Express, Equitas, Standard Chartered and Unity weren’t in that list.
