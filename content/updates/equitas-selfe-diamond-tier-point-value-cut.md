Equitas has quietly changed its top Xchange Rewards tier on the SELFE card. Diamond is now called "The Pinnacle of Luxury" on the rewards portal. Points are now worth half what they used to be, so the top tier drops from about 10% valueback to 5%. The new values are in Equitas's Privilege Program T&C dated November 2026.

## What's changing

| What's changing | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Flights, hotels, bus, vouchers, merchandise** | 1 RP = ₹1 | 1 RP = ₹0.50 |
| **Cashback / Insta redemption** (pay by points) | 1 RP = ₹0.50 | 1 RP = ₹0.30 |
| **Spend to reach the tier** | ₹1,00,000 a month for 3 months | No change |

The spend is counted net of refunds, every month of one calendar quarter, and the tier then applies for the next quarter. Rent, wallet, fuel, insurance, utilities and EMIs all count.

## SELFE point values in every tier now

| Tier (monthly spend) | Flights, hotels, vouchers | Cashback / Insta |
|---|---|---|
| Blue (base) | ₹0.25 | ₹0.15 |
| Silver (₹20K+) | ₹0.35 | ₹0.15 |
| Gold (₹40K+) | ₹0.35 | ₹0.20 |
| Platinum (₹60K+) | ₹0.40 | ₹0.25 |
| Diamond (₹1L+) | ₹0.50 | ₹0.30 |

The other Diamond perks stay: 0% forex markup (refunded as cashback), three ₹250 movie vouchers, three ₹500 Amazon vouchers and golf green fees each quarter.

The portal also says the MakeMyTrip one-way flight and room night vouchers for Diamond are out of stock. Equitas says fresh stock comes in December 2026, and you'll get 10 days to claim once it tells you.

## Overnight, with no notice

Banks can't change terms like this overnight. Under RBI's credit card rules, they have to give at least 30 days' notice before a change. I use the SELFE card myself, so I've mailed Equitas about this. I'll update this post when they reply.

## What I'd do

- If you're in the top tier with points saved up, redeem on flights, hotels or vouchers, not cashback. That still gets you ₹0.50 a point against ₹0.30.
- Each redemption order costs ₹99, so redeem in one go, not in small lots.
- ₹1 lakh a month for 5% back is a big ask now. If SELFE was your main card only for that 10%, look at moving those spends.
