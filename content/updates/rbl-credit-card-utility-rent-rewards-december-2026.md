This is a revaluation, the opposite of what most banks have done this year. RBL Bank emailed cardholders on 23 November 2026: from 1 December 2026, utility and rent spends on all RBL Bank credit cards earn rewards and count toward card benefits, and the 1% fee on them goes away.

## What's changing

| What's changing | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Reward points / cashback** | Not earned | Earned |
| **Milestone benefits** | Not counted | Counted |
| **Spend-based lounge access** | Not counted | Counted |
| **Membership fee waiver** | Not counted | Counted |
| **1% transaction fee** | Charged | Removed |

The change covers two merchant codes: utility (MCC 4900) and rental (MCC 6513). Milestones, lounge access and fee waivers only count where your card has them. Everything else follows your own card's terms, so the earn rate is whatever your card gives on regular spends.

## What I'd do

- From 1 December, pay electricity, gas and water bills on your RBL card. They now earn and count toward the annual fee waiver.
- If you pay rent by card, check the merchant code on your statement. Only spends coded 6513 get this.
- Wait till 1 December for big bill or rent payments, so they don't pick up the old 1% fee.
