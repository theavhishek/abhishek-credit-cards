Good news for once. Amazon has raised how much you can spend on Amazon Pay Gift Cards with a credit or debit card. If you load gift cards to earn card cashback or reward points, you now get a lot more room. Bookmark this one.

## What's changing

| Period | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **30 days** | ₹30,000 | ₹50,000 |
| **60 days** | ₹40,000 | ₹1,00,000 |
| **90 days** | ₹50,000 | ₹1,50,000 |
| **1 year** | ₹3,60,000 | ₹6,00,000 |

In one order you can buy up to 5 gift cards of up to ₹10,000 each, so ₹50,000 at once. That's a full month's limit in a single order.

## Why your card may still be blocked

- You've already crossed one of the limits above on that card. The 60 and 90 day limits count too, not just the monthly one.
- The limit is tracked on your Amazon account and on the card itself. Adding the same card to another Amazon account won't get you around it straight away.

## What I'd do

- Keep a note of how much you've loaded on each card, so you stay under ₹50,000 in any 30 days.
- Hit the limit on a card? Pay the rest by UPI.
- Have more than one card that rewards gift card loads? Rotate between them.
- Before a big load, check Amazon's help page linked below, since Amazon can change these limits without notice.
