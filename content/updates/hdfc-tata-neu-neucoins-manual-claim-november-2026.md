From **25 November 2026**, NeuCoins earned on Tata Neu HDFC Bank credit cards (Plus and Infinity) no longer move to your Tata Neu account on their own. You have to **claim them in the Tata Neu app**. Unclaimed NeuCoins expire 12 months after the statement they were earned in.

Earning rates don't change. Only the claim and redemption process does.

## At a glance

| Feature | Before 25 Nov 2026 | From 25 Nov 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| NeuCoins transfer | Automatic | Manual claim in the Tata Neu app |
| Minimum claim | Not applicable | 100 NeuCoins |
| Daily limit | Not applicable | 3 successful claims |
| When available | Credited within 7 working days of the statement | Claimable within 7 working days of the statement |
| Claim deadline | Not applicable | 12 months from the statement date |
| Validity after credit | Existing Tata Neu rules | Another 12 months, under Tata Neu rules |

## How to claim

1. Open the **Tata Neu app** and log in with the primary cardholder's bank-registered mobile number.
2. Go to **NeuCard**.
3. Tap **Claim NeuCoins**. It shows your claimable balance.
4. Follow the on-screen steps to submit the claim.
5. A successful claim reaches your Tata Neu Membership account within **7 working days**.

Credited NeuCoins and their expiry dates show in the Tata NeuCoins section of the app.

## Claim rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum | 100 NeuCoins per claim. HDFC may lower it; the app shows the current limit |
| Daily limit | 3 successful claims, primary cardholder only |
| Claimable from | Within 7 working days of the statement date |
| Claim window | 12 months from that statement date |
| After the deadline | Unclaimed NeuCoins expire |
| After a claim | Another 12 months of validity in Tata Neu |

## HDFC's example timeline

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Statement generated | 15 Dec 2026 |
| NeuCoins claimable | By 22 Dec 2026 |
| Last date to claim | 14 Dec 2027 |
| If credited to Tata Neu on 15 or 27 Dec 2026 | Valid until 31 Dec 2027 |

## Two expiry clocks

- **On the card:** 12 months from the statement date to claim.
- **In Tata Neu:** 12 more months after the claimed NeuCoins are credited, under Tata Neu Rewards Programme rules.

## NeuCoins from statements before 25 November 2026

- **Credit:** automatic, within 7 working days of the statement, as before.
- **Validity:** existing rules continue. Example: credited on 5 or 31 Aug 2026, valid until 31 Aug 2027.
- **Action needed:** none. You don't claim these.

## Changing your statement date moves the deadline

| Case | Last date to claim |
|---|---|
| Statement on 15 Dec 2026, date unchanged | 14 Dec 2027 |
| Statement date changed from the 15th to the 10th before Dec 2027 | 9 Dec 2027 |

Check unclaimed NeuCoins before you change your billing cycle.

## Expiry warnings

- **Card statement:** highlights NeuCoins expiring in the next 30 or 60 days.
- **Tata Neu app:** shows credited NeuCoins and their expiry dates.
- **Welcome and promotional NeuCoins:** keep the validity told to you when they were given.

## Special cases

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Claim option missing (new, upgraded or replacement card, or new mobile number) | Verify the card: Tata Neu app → NeuCard → Already have a NeuCard / Verify your NeuCard |
| Card blocked or closed | Claims not allowed |
| Card unblocked by the bank | Claims open again within up to 3 working days |
| Card reissued (lost or stolen) | Unclaimed NeuCoins move to the new card within 7 working days, then become claimable within 7 working days of the next statement |
| Variant change (e.g. Plus to Infinity) | Same as a reissue |
| App balance lower than the statement | The app subtracts claims made since the statement, and later reversals |

## Where NeuCoins can be used

**1 NeuCoin = ₹1** off eligible purchases on Tata brands through Tata Neu.

| Redemption | Brands |
|---|---|
| Available | BigBasket, Croma, Tata CLiQ, Tata 1mg, Westside, IHCL hotels, Air India Express, Titan |
| Not available for now | Air India, Tata Play, Cult.fit, bill payments on Tata Neu |

If the card is active and claims still fail, send a screenshot of the error.

## What to do now

- Check your claimable NeuCoins after every statement and claim them once they appear.
- Watch the 30 and 60-day expiry warnings on your statement.
- Claim everything before you close, block, upgrade or change the statement date of your card.

From 25 November 2026, earning NeuCoins isn't enough. You have to claim them too. HDFC's circular was last updated on 21 October 2026; read it below.
