Punjab National Bank has revised its credit card charges from **1 November 2026**. Three changes matter most: a new fee on large utility bill payments, a fee on every reward points redemption, and higher late payment fees. The changes apply to PNB Visa and RuPay credit cards.

## At a glance

| Charge | Until 31 Oct 2026 | From 1 Nov 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Utility payments above ₹50,000 a cycle | NIL | 1% of total utility payments, max ₹2,000 per transaction |
| Reward points redemption | Free | ₹99 + GST per request |
| Highest late payment fee | ₹750 | ₹1,200 |
| Late fee waived up to | ₹1,000 due | ₹500 due |

All revised charges are exclusive of GST.

## 1. Utility bill payments: 1% above ₹50,000

- **Fee:** 1% of total utility payments once they cross ₹50,000 in a billing cycle.
- **Cap:** ₹2,000 per transaction.
- **Threshold:** counted across all utility payments in the billing cycle, not per transaction.

PNB's notice puts the fee on the total utility amount, not only on the part above ₹50,000. It doesn't explain how the fee is split across individual transactions.

## 2. Reward points redemption: ₹99 per request

| Particulars | Charge |
|---|---|
| Existing fee | Free |
| New fee | ₹99 + GST |
| Applies to | Each redemption request |

This cuts the value of your points most on small redemptions.

## 3. Late payment fees go up

| Total amount due | New fee |
|---|---|
| ₹500 or less | NIL |
| ₹501 to ₹1,000 | ₹500 |
| ₹1,001 to ₹10,000 | ₹750 |
| ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 | ₹1,000 |
| Above ₹50,000 | ₹1,200 |

PNB's notice lists ₹10,000 in two slabs, so the fee at exactly ₹10,000 needs the bank to clarify.

Until now, no late fee applied up to ₹1,000 due, and the highest fee was ₹750.

- **FD-backed cards:** the late fee stays at 30% of the minimum amount due, with a minimum of ₹200 and a maximum of ₹500 per billing cycle.
- **Luxura cards:** have their own provisions in the notice.

## What to do now

- If you pay big utility bills on a PNB card, keep the cycle's total at or under ₹50,000, or move the extra to another card.
- Batch your reward redemptions so the ₹99 fee is paid less often.
- Set up autopay for at least the minimum due, since the late fee now reaches ₹1,200.

Read PNB's full notice below before 1 November.
