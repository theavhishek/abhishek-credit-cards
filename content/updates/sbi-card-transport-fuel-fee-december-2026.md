## How the fees work

| Charge | Trigger | What you pay |
|---|---|---|
| Transportation | Any single transaction above ₹30,000 | 1% of the full transaction. A ₹40,000 payment incurs ₹400; ₹30,000 incurs nothing. |
| Fuel | Total fuel spending above ₹50,000 in one billing cycle | 1% of all fuel spending that cycle, not just the amount over the threshold. ₹60,000 incurs ₹600; ₹50,000 incurs nothing. |

## Which payments count

SBI Card identifies payments by merchant category code (MCC). The notice lists **4111** (commuter transport), **4131** (bus lines) and **4784** (tolls and bridges) for transportation. For fuel, it lists **5172** (petroleum products), **5541** (service stations), **5542** (automated fuel dispensers) and **5983** (fuel dealers).

Card networks define these MCCs and may change them without telling cardholders, SBI Card says.

## What remains unclear

The notice does not say whether GST is added to either fee, whether either has a cap, or how the fuel fee interacts with a card’s fuel surcharge waiver. Confirm those points with SBI Card before relying on a waiver or estimating your final charge.

## Before paying

Check the merchant’s MCC and your fuel total for the current billing cycle. If a payment may cross a threshold, consider another payment method or card. For clarification, call SBI Card at [1860 180 1290](tel:18601801290) or 39 02 02 02 with your local STD code.
