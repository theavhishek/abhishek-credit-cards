SBI Card is adding two new fees from **1 December 2026**: 1% on large transportation payments and 1% on heavy fuel spending. They apply to SBI credit cards.

## At a glance

| Charge | When it applies | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Transportation | A single transaction above ₹30,000 | 1% of that transaction |
| Fuel | Fuel total above ₹50,000 in a billing cycle | 1% of the cycle's total fuel spends |

## 1. Transportation: 1% above ₹30,000 per transaction

- **Fee:** 1% of the full transaction amount.
- **Trigger:** each transaction above ₹30,000, checked one by one.
- **Smaller payments:** a transaction of ₹30,000 or less has no fee.
- **MCCs:** 4111, 4784, 4131.

## 2. Fuel: 1% once the cycle crosses ₹50,000

- **Fee:** 1% of all fuel spends in the cycle, not only the part above ₹50,000.
- **Trigger:** the total of all fuel transactions in one billing cycle above ₹50,000.
- **MCCs:** 5172, 5541, 5542, 5983.

## Examples

| Spend | Fee |
|---|---|
| One transport payment of ₹30,000 | Nil |
| One transport payment of ₹40,000 | ₹400 |
| Fuel of ₹50,000 in a cycle | Nil |
| Fuel of ₹60,000 in a cycle | ₹600 |

## Which spends count

| MCC | Standard MCC name | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| 4111 | Local and suburban commuter transport | Transportation |
| 4131 | Bus lines | Transportation |
| 4784 | Tolls and bridge fees | Transportation |
| 5172 | Petroleum and petroleum products | Fuel |
| 5541 | Service stations | Fuel |
| 5542 | Automated fuel dispensers | Fuel |
| 5983 | Fuel dealers | Fuel |

SBI's notice lists only the codes. The card networks set the MCCs and can change them without SBI Card telling you.

## What SBI's notice doesn't say

- Whether GST is added on top of the fee.
- Whether there is a maximum fee.
- How the fuel fee works with your card's fuel surcharge waiver.

## What to do now

- Keep each transport payment at or under ₹30,000 where you can.
- If your fuel spends near ₹50,000 a cycle, move the rest to another card.
- For questions, call SBI Card at [1860 180 1290](tel:18601801290) or 39 02 02 02 with your local STD code.

Read SBI Card's notice below before 1 December.
