ICICI Bank is running a Bollywood spend campaign for its Visa credit cardholders in November 2026: **"A Bigger Spend. A Bigger Bollywood Moment!"** It is a spend ranking, not a lucky draw. Spending ₹2 lakh only gets you into the ranking.

## Offer at a glance

| Detail | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Bank and network | ICICI Bank, Visa |
| Offer period | 1 to 30 November 2026 |
| Minimum spend | ₹2,00,000 in eligible retail spends, on one card |
| Top 5 spenders | Meet Bollywood stars |
| Next 500 spenders | Two red-carpet movie screening tickets each |
| Registration | Mandatory, missed call to **02269445464** |
| Winners contacted | 7 to 20 December 2026 |
| Not eligible | Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card |

## Rewards

| Rank | Reward | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 5 | Meet Bollywood stars | Travel to the venue at your own cost; stars not named |
| 6 to 505 | 2 screening tickets each (up to 1,000 tickets) | City allocated by the bank, subject to availability |

- Meet and greet is subject to the event's arrangements.
- Rewards can't be transferred or exchanged for cash or anything else.

### Screening cities

Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow and Panipat.

- You may not get your preferred city.
- The terms don't name the film, the screening dates or the cinemas.

Treat the SRK and KING link as a possibility, not a benefit.

## How to register

1. Check that you have an eligible ICICI Bank Visa credit card.
2. Give a missed call to **02269445464** from your ICICI-registered mobile number.
3. Spend at least ₹2,00,000 in eligible retail spends on that one card between 1 and 30 November 2026.
4. If you rank in the top 505, ICICI Bank contacts you on your registered details.

- A missed call from any other number doesn't count.
- The missed call is also your consent to campaign messages on WhatsApp from ICICI Bank.
- The terms give no separate last date to register.

## Eligible cards

- ICICI Bank Visa credit cards that are eligible under the campaign.
- **Excluded:** Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card.
- ICICI Bank employees and their family members can't take part.
- Spends on two cards can't be combined. Each card is counted on its own.
- The terms don't list eligible card variants, so don't assume every ICICI Visa card qualifies. Check with ICICI Bank ([ICICI Bank contacts](/bank-support)).

## What counts

**Counts:** eligible retail spends on the eligible card in the offer period, after the transactions settle.

**Doesn't count:**

- EMI transactions
- Cash withdrawals
- Reversed, refunded, disputed or cancelled transactions
- Any other transaction the campaign rules treat as ineligible

The terms have no merchant-category list. They don't say whether wallet loads, rent, insurance, utilities, gift cards or government payments count, so don't count on them.

## How winners are picked

| Step | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Qualify | Registered by missed call, eligible Visa card, ₹2 lakh+ eligible spends |
| Rank | By eligible spends after settlement, highest first |
| Tie | Whoever reached the qualifying spend first ranks higher |
| Result | Ranks 1 to 5 meet and greet; 6 to 505 screening tickets |

**₹2 lakh is the entry bar, not a prize.** If 505 people spend more, ₹2 lakh wins nothing.

## When winners hear

- Between **7 and 20 December 2026**.
- By email, SMS, phone call or WhatsApp, on your registered details.
- The terms don't promise a public winners list or a set announcement day.

## Other terms

- The bank can disqualify fraudulent, abusive or suspicious transactions.
- ICICI Bank can change, extend or withdraw the campaign and its rewards.
- The bank's decisions on eligibility, ranking, selection and fulfilment are final.

## Is it worth spending ₹2 lakh?

Only if the spend was already planned. There's no cashback or points for reaching ₹2 lakh, and the reward goes only to the top 505. Don't buy things or borrow just to chase a rank.
