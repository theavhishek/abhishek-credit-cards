## What changes

| Change | Now | Revised | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complimentary INOX movie offers | Available | Discontinued | 15 Dec 2026 |
| Complimentary insurance cover | Included | Discontinued | 15 Dec 2026 |
| Card replacement fee | ₹200 + GST | ₹300 + GST | 11 Dec 2026 |
| Priority Pass replacement | Free | ₹300 + GST | 11 Dec 2026 |
| DCC fee, business and corporate cards | 1% | 3.50% | 25 Nov 2026 |
| Replacement fee, business and corporate cards | ₹200 + GST | ₹300 + GST | 25 Nov 2026 |

## Good to know

- **INOX offers end on:** Coral, Rubyx, Expressions, CSK, HPCL Super Saver, MakeMyTrip and some Emirates variants.
- **Air accident and related insurance ends on:** Rubyx, Sapphiro, Emeralde, Diamant and Times Black. Cancellation For Any Reason (CFAR) cover also ends on some Emeralde variants and Times Black.
- **Personal and air accident cover ends on:** Parakram and Parakram Select.
- **Replacement fee:** ₹354 with 18% GST. Emeralde Private Metal, Times Black and 1838 Reserve are not affected.
- **Priority Pass fee:** applies to eligible premium and travel cards, such as Sapphiro, Emeralde and Times Black.
- **DCC:** charged on rupee payments made abroad or to merchants registered outside India. Personal cards are not part of this change.
- **Your card:** each change covers only some cards, so check the full list in ICICI's notice.
