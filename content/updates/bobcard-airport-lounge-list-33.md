## Good to know

- **The cut:** NDTV Profit, citing LoungeInsider, reported that the list had 83 lounges before. That is 50 fewer, a cut of about 60%.
- **Bengaluru:** reports say premium lounges at Bengaluru Airport were dropped.
- **International lounges:** none are on this domestic list. That doesn't mean separate international lounge benefits on some BOBCARD variants have ended.
- **Free visits:** still depend on your card, its spend condition and its visit limit.
- **Before you fly:** check that your lounge is on the list. BOBCARD can change the list and card eligibility at any time.
