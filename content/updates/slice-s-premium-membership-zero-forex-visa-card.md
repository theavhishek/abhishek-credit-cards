slice has started teasing slice S, its upcoming premium membership. I asked slice support about it, and they confirmed it's launching soon. Here's what slice has said so far, and what it hasn't.

## What slice S offers

| Benefit | What slice says |
|---|---|
| **Savings** | 115% of the RBI repo rate on money you set aside in your slice atom |
| **Credit card** | A Visa credit card with zero forex markup on international spends |
| **Travel** | 5x monies on travel bookings |
| **More** | Further benefits will be revealed at launch |

## The card

The card in slice's poster is a pink card with **Visa Signature** printed on it, so it looks like a Visa Signature variant. It may be backed by a fixed deposit, but that's my guess, not something slice has confirmed.

## Still unknown

- Membership price
- Who is eligible
- The card's fees, limits and full reward terms
- Launch date

slice support says eligibility and pricing will be shared when slice S goes live.

## What I'd do

If you spend abroad often, keep an eye on this one. A zero forex card with travel rewards could be worth it, but wait for the membership price before you plan around it. I'll update this post once slice announces the full details.
