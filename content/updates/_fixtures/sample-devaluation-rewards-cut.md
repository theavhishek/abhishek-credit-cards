This is sample text for layout testing. Example Bank has told Platinum cardholders that two categories stop earning reward points from the first day of next month.

## What changes

- Rent payments made through any app earn no points.
- Wallet loads earn no points.
- The monthly cap on grocery points falls from 2,000 to 1,000.

## Who it affects

Anyone who used the card mainly for rent will see the biggest drop. If most of your spend is on dining and travel, nothing changes for you, because those categories keep their current rates and caps.

> The new rules apply to transactions that post on or after the effective date, not the date you swipe.

## What to do now

1. Check your last three statements to see how much of your spend was rent or wallet loads.
2. If it was more than a third, compare the card's value against a card that still rewards rent.
3. Ask the bank for a fee waiver or a product change before the annual fee is due.
