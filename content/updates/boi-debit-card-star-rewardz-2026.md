## Points per transaction

Every BOI debit card earns Star Points on POS and online spends. The rate depends on the size of each transaction.

| One transaction | Points per ₹100 | Value at ₹0.25 a point |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₹5,000 | 1 | 0.25% |
| ₹5,001 to ₹10,000 | 1.5 | 0.375% |
| Above ₹10,000 | 2 | 0.5% |
| Above ₹10,000 on RuPay Select | 2 + 3 bonus | more than 0.5% |

## Good to know

- **Monthly cap:** 10,000 points per customer ID (about ₹2,500).
- **Monthly bonus:** 100 extra points when the month's spends cross ₹50,000. They don't count towards the cap.
- **Bill payments:** earn a few flat points per bill, with a small monthly cap, not the slab rates above.
- **Validity:** points expire after 3 years. You need 100 points before you can redeem.
- **Redeem:** on [boistarrewardz.com](https://www.boistarrewardz.com) for vouchers, flights, products and more. Points usually show up within 3 to 7 days.
- **Tip:** put big single payments above ₹10,000 on the card to get the top slab.

## RuPay Select Debit Card

- **Fee:** ₹800 + GST (₹944) to issue, and the same every year.
- **Eligibility:** any BOI savings account except BSBDA. The account can be opened online.
- **Benefits:** RuPay Select perks, including domestic lounge visits (one a quarter after ₹5,000 of spends the previous quarter), accident cover and wellness offers like gym, spa and golf.
- **Tip:** as a PSU bank, BOI is usually quickest at the branch. Open the account and ask for the RuPay Select card on the spot.
